## What this proves

The whole trainer's forward — every kernel, the 24-layer model assembly, the LoRA path, the readout and the
soft-probability writer — runs on the eGPU through tinycudart/tinycublas/tinynv and lands **within 0.0011 of the same
binary built with real nvcc + cuBLAS on gpubox**, over all 160 rows, with no argmax flip anywhere. M1–M5 checked each
piece against a CPU reference; this is the first check of the assembled trainer against the other build, on real data.
It also shows the shim's TF32 path is not a different arithmetic: the card's 0.0011 sits between gpubox's own
tf32-vs-exact spread (0.0001 in CE, ~0.0001 in |dp|) and the 0.01 gate, i.e. the shim adds no error of its own beyond
the mode it runs in.
