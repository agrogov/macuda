Short-circuited per this card's own instruction: read `notes/J48.md` line 2 before anything else.


J48 re-profiled the attention core after J44 removed the head-major transposes and measured it at
**2.71% of a TF32 step / 5.24% of an exact step** — below the 4% TF32 threshold that would justify a
flash rewrite (J38's pre-J44 ceiling was 10.1% / 17.1%). J49 therefore skipped the oracle (`ref/attn.c`)
and marked the GPU checks in `test/k_attn.c` unbuilt, and this card's instruction says a NOT LANDED
line 2 on J48 means skip. Rule 8 agrees: a rewrite that removes an L x L score matrix worth ~2.7% of
a TF32 step is not "worth it", and it cannot be bit-identical in either class, so it would fail the
harmlessness gates as well.

## What was not changed

- `kernels/attn.cu`, `kernels/attn.h`, `src/layer_attn.c`, `test/k_attn.c`: untouched. No `JT_ATTN_FLASH`
  toggle, no `attn: flash` startup line, no backward recomputation path, no dynamic-shared-memory
  (`cudaFuncSetAttribute`) work. `JT_ATTN_BATCH` (J45) is likewise untouched and keeps its default path.
- No `ab.sh` / `ab.sh --same`, no `parity-eval.sh`, no `parity-steps.sh`, no gpubox build, no card run:
  there was nothing to measure, and rule 8 forbids landing on expectation rather than numbers.
- `git status --short` is empty apart from this note, so there is no `notes/J50.wip.patch` to save.

## Numbers this card would have recorded (none measured here)

For the hand-off: the only measurements that exist are J48's shares of a step — **2.71% TF32 /
5.24% exact** at batch 8 x accum 1, 20 steps, median of steps 3..20, 3 runs per configuration. The
M8 follow-up order therefore puts flash attention behind items 1 (bf16 weights), 2 (chunked GDN) and
ahead of nothing in particular; a future card should only revive it above J48's 4% threshold, i.e. at
a shape where attention is a larger share of the step (longer L, smaller batch).
