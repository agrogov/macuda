`make shim` log starts with that container's CUDA 13.0.3 banner, and `/tmp/tinycc_*.fatbin` files were produced. Nothing
was down.

## What this does and does not prove

* **Proves** (plan M7.1): the two builds of one source still agree, nothing in the trainer calls a cudart/cuBLAS entry
  the shim lacks, every kernel launches through the `<<<>>>` ABI the shim lowers, and the whole trainer start-up path —
  `gemm_init` → `lora_alloc` → `model_load` (321 tensors, every dtype and dimension checked by `st_get`) → `jt_ctx_alloc`
  → the eval forward → the `pred-*.jsonl`/`report.json` writers — runs to completion on the null device with no error.
* **Does not prove** any number. The null device reports launches instead of executing them, so the eval's CE/agreement
  above are meaningless by construction; parity is M7.2/M7.3 (cards, with `nv_shim_step.sh` and the user's OK).
* Peak memory reads 0.00 GB because the shim's `cudaMemGetInfo` reports total−free and the null device reports all of
  its budget free; the real figures are in the `model:`/`ctx:` lines (1.99 GB fp32 weights + 0.51 GB bf16 embedding +
  0.11 GB arena).
